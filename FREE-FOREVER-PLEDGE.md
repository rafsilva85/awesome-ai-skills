# SkillFlow Free Forever Pledge

## Our Promise

SkillFlow commits to keeping its core marketplace **free forever** for individual developers and small teams.

### What's Always Free

- **Browse & Search** — Full access to the entire skill catalog
- **Trust Scores** — Every skill's security, reliability, and performance ratings
- **Install Skills** — Download and use any free skill
- **Submit Skills** — List your skills on the marketplace
- **MCP Server** — Programmatic access via MCP protocol
- **CLI Tool** — `skillflow search` and `skillflow install`
- **VS Code Extension** — Browse skills in your editor
- **API Access** — Up to 1,000 requests/day

### Why Free?

The AI skills ecosystem is fragmented. Developers waste hours finding, evaluating, and integrating tools. We believe **curation should be a public good**, not a paywall.

Our business model is built on:
1. **Premium skills** — Paid skills from verified creators (we take a commission)
2. **Enterprise features** — Team management, private catalogs, SLA support
3. **Sponsorships** — Featured placements for tool vendors

### The Pledge

We, the SkillFlow team, pledge that:

> The core SkillFlow marketplace will remain **free and accessible** to individual developers and small teams (up to 5 people) **forever**. We will never put basic search, browse, trust scores, or skill installation behind a paywall.
